# News notes — Saturday 21 November 2026

Researched on Sunday 22 November 2026 (the day was complete). Every story below
was first reported on 21 November 2026 and is confirmed by at least two

## 1. Iran strikes U.S. bases in Bahrain (LEAD)
- Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they struck two U.S. military positions in
  Bahrain, at al-Riffa and Awali. Bahrain said the attack hit a civilian
  industrial facility and started a fire, with no injuries, and that its air
  defences intercepted other attacks. Iran also said it downed a U.S. drone
  near Qeshm Island; the U.S. denied Iran's claim that it hit an F-16.
- Two tankers were also struck by unknown projectiles east of Khasab, Oman,
  on 21 November, with no casualties (UK Maritime Trade Operations).
- Sources: Associated Press (21 Nov 2026, via WPXI world desk); Reuters
  (21 Nov 2026, "Bahrain says Iranian attack hits industrial facility");
  OneTurn day briefing for 21 Nov 2026.

## 2. Mexico captures cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro"
- Mexican special forces captured Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as
  "Chapo Isidro", a Beltran Leyva leader on the FBI's ten most-wanted list,
  in a raid in Guasave, Sinaloa, with help from U.S. intelligence. Gunfights,
  fires and road blockades followed; at least 11 people were killed.
- Sources: Associated Press (21 Nov 2026); Reuters (21 Nov 2026, "Mexico
  captures top drug lord 'El Chapo Isidro'"); BBC (21 Nov 2026).

## 3. Russian missile hits Zelenskyy's hometown; weapons land in Poland
- A Russian ballistic missile struck the centre of Kryvyi Rih, President
  Zelenskyy's hometown, killing a 7-year-old boy and a 20-year-old woman and
  wounding 60 people, including 6 children, in a double-tap attack.
- Russian missiles and a drone also landed in Poland during the largest
  November barrage on Ukraine; Poland called it an intentional provocation.
- Sources: Associated Press (21 Nov 2026); AAP (21 Nov 2026); Reuters
  (21 Nov 2026, via Google News roundup).

## 4. COP31 climate talks run into overtime in Antalya

  deal and adaptation finance was unresolved. Venue crews were already
  boxing up as negotiators chased a final agreement.
- Sources: Associated Press (21 Nov 2026); Reuters (21 Nov 2026, "COP31
  climate summit risks failure as talks go deep into overtime"); BBC

## 6. Bitcoin plunges to a two-year low, then rebounds 9%
- Bitcoin fell to about $55,130 on Saturday 21 November, its lowest since
  September 2024, after more than $2.5 billion of leveraged positions were
  liquidated in 24 hours. It then rebounded more than 8-9% to reclaim
  $60,000 the same day.
- Sources: CoinDesk (21 Nov 2026, 12:50 p.m. EST); Reuters (21 Nov 2026,
  "Bitcoin rises after falling to two-year low"); KuCoin News (21 Nov 2026).

## 7. Dengue breakthrough: special mosquitoes cut infections 69%
- A four-year randomised trial in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, found that
  neighbourhoods with Wolbachia-carrying mosquitoes had about 69% fewer
  dengue and chikungunya infections in schoolchildren (5,757 children aged
  6-11), measured by blood tests. Results were presented on Saturday
  21 November at the American Society of Tropical Medicine and Hygiene
  meeting; not yet peer reviewed.
- Sources: Associated Press (21 Nov 2026); Tech Times (21 Nov 2026);
  Gulf News / AP (21 Nov 2026).

## 8. Boat capsizes in Nigeria; at least 24 dead
- A boat carrying mourners to a burial ceremony capsized in Bayelsa State,
  southern Nigeria, on Friday; emergency services said on Saturday that at
  least 24 people had died and 24 others were rescued. The boat was
  travelling from Ayama to Ukubie when it developed a mechanical fault.
- Sources: Associated Press (21 Nov 2026, via Reflector/Netscape);
  BusinessDay Nigeria (21 Nov 2026); Finwire (21 Nov 2026).
